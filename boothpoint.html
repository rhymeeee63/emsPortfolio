What's inside

Everything a studio actually runs on, in one system.

Not a spreadsheet plus five apps duct-taped together — one login, one database, one bill.

Bookings & Calendar

Every inquiry tracked from first message to event day, with separate capacity limits for photographer and photobooth teams so double-bookings never sneak through.

Payments & Paperwork

Percentage or fixed deposits, balance-due tracking, and PDF quotations and invoices generated straight from the booking — no separate invoicing app.

Public Booking Tracker

Clients follow their own status — inquiry, confirmed, deposit paid, gallery delivered — through one link you send them. No login, no app to install.

Staff & Equipment

Assign photographers and photobooth crews per event, track payouts per team, and keep tabs on gear without a spreadsheet.

Mail That Doesn't Quit

Confirmations and reminders send through your main provider. If it ever fails, BoothPoint switches to a backup provider automatically for 24 hours — no missed emails.

Make It Yours

Pick your color palette and homepage layout — Split, Centered, or Full-Bleed Photo — and write your own About page and FAQ. One codebase, your studio's look.

Activity & Audit Logs

Full audit trail of admin actions, status transitions, staff assignments, and payment logs with precise timestamps for complete operational accountability.
